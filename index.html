📖 What Exactly Does It Do?

The core concept is simple: when a short-term moving average (e.g., 20-day) crosses above a long-term moving average (e.g., 50-day), it's a buy signal. When it crosses below, it's a sell signal.

But which numbers - which moving-average lengths - work the best? Most traders guess. This application doesn't guess. It systematically tests every sensible combination (e.g., 5/15, 5/20, 5/30, 10/20, 10/30, 15/30, and hundreds more) and ranks them by performance.

🧠 Why The "Deflated Sharpe" Is Important

Many tools just tell you the best-performing strategy - but that's often just luck. With hundreds of trials, some will look great purely by chance.

The deflated Sharpe ratio inside this tool adjusts the results to account for how many strategies were tested. If a strategy looks good but is likely just due to chance, the tool tells you honestly. That means you get trustworthy results you can actually use.
